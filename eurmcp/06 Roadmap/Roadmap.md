## Execution rule

Finish the P0 trust layer before expanding discovery or deployment. A wider server with weaker evidence is a regression.

## Priority meaning

- P0: required to support the best-in-class accuracy claim.
- P1: required to match or exceed the strongest documented EU-law MCP workflows.
- P2: useful after the trust and production layers are complete.

## Status flow

proposed → specified → implementing → verified → released
